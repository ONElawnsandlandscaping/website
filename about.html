About Us

Who We Are

We're Jake and Ayden, the owners and operators of ONE Lawns & Landscaping. We started small right here in our own neighborhood in Yukon. We've been working to grow our business and build a reputation for quality work and reliable service.

We provide a variety of lawn care and landscaping services, from routine mowing and lawn maintenance to flower beds, mulch, decorative rock, and other outdoor projects. No matter the size of the job, we want every customer to receive dependable service and work they can be satisfied with.

Our Business Standard

Our standard is simple: Anything Worth Doing, Is Worth Doing Right. We believe quality comes down to the details, whether we're mowing a yard or completing a larger landscaping project. We work to meet our customers' expectations, take care of their properties, and do the job right.

We also believe communication and reliability are important parts of running a business. We want our customers to know what to expect and feel confident in the work we're doing. As we continue to grow, we're always looking for ways to improve our services and maintain the standards our business was built on.
