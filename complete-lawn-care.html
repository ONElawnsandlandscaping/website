Fertilization

Fertilization provides nutrients that can support healthy lawn growth, color, and overall lawn condition. The timing and amount needed depend on the grass type, current lawn conditions, seasonal needs, and the fertilizer being used.

Bermuda grass grows differently throughout the year, so its fertilization needs change with the seasons. Applications should be based on active growth and lawn conditions rather than using the same schedule year-round.

Spring — After Green-Up

Once Bermuda grass has greened up and is actively growing, fertilization may help support healthy new growth. Timing depends on temperatures, lawn conditions, and the product's directions.

Summer — Active Growth

Bermuda grass typically grows actively during warm weather. Fertilization may be appropriate when the lawn needs additional nutrients. Weather, watering, and lawn condition should be considered before applying fertilizer.

Fall — Growth Slows

As temperatures cool, Bermuda grass begins slowing its growth. Fertilization needs change during this period, and late-season applications should be considered carefully.

Winter — Dormant Grass

Dormant Bermuda grass generally does not need routine nitrogen fertilization. Lawn needs can be reassessed as warmer weather approaches and the grass prepares to green up again.

Important: These are general seasonal guidelines, not a guaranteed application schedule. Fertilizer selection, timing, and amounts depend on lawn conditions, weather, soil needs, and product label directions.

Spot Spraying & Weed Control

Spot spraying targets weeds in specific areas around the property. Depending on the conditions and the product being used, these areas may include flower beds, sidewalk cracks, driveway cracks, and other appropriate localized spots where weeds are growing.

Spot spraying will be performed during each service visit when needed, targeting appropriate areas where weeds are present. Areas without weeds may not need treatment on that visit.

Please note: Spot spraying targets weeds in specific areas when needed during each service visit. We do not offer full-lawn weed control treatments.

Treatment Guidelines

Every lawn and property is different. Treatment decisions depend on the weeds present, the surrounding grass and landscape, weather, and the intended use of the product.

  • Products are used according to their label directions.
  • Weather and site conditions can affect application timing.
  • Spot spraying is performed where needed, not automatically everywhere.
  • Results can vary based on weed type, lawn condition, and other factors.

If you have questions about treatment for a particular area of your property, you can include those details when requesting your lawn care plan.
