Fertilization

Fertilization provides nutrients that can support healthy lawn growth, color, and overall lawn condition. The timing and amount needed depend on grass type, lawn conditions, seasonal needs, and the product used.

Bermuda grass grows differently throughout the year, so its fertilization needs change with the seasons. Applications should match active growth and lawn conditions rather than follow the same schedule year-round.

Spring — After Green-Up

Once Bermuda grass has greened up and is actively growing, fertilization may help support healthy new growth. Timing depends on temperatures, lawn conditions, and product directions.

Summer — Active Growth

During active growth, Bermuda grass may benefit from additional nutrients when needed. Applications should account for the lawn's condition, watering, weather, and the product's directions.

Fall — Slowing Growth

As temperatures cool and growth slows, fertilization needs change. Late-season applications should be considered carefully rather than automatically continuing a summer schedule.

Winter — Dormant Grass

Dormant Bermuda grass generally does not need routine nitrogen fertilization. The lawn's needs can be reassessed as warmer weather approaches and spring green-up begins.

Important: These are general seasonal guidelines, not a guaranteed application schedule. Fertilizer selection, timing, and amounts depend on lawn needs, weather, soil conditions, and product label directions.

Spot Spraying & Weed Control

Spot spraying targets weeds in specific areas around the property. Depending on the conditions and product being used, these areas may include flower beds, sidewalk cracks, driveway cracks, and other appropriate localized spots where weeds are growing.

Spot spraying will be performed during each service visit when needed, targeting appropriate areas where weeds are present. Areas without weeds may not need treatment on that visit.

Please note: Spot spraying targets weeds in specific areas when needed during each service visit. We do not offer full-lawn weed control treatments.

Treatment Guidelines

Every lawn and property is different. Treatment decisions depend on the weeds present, grass and landscape conditions, weather, and the treatment product's intended use.

  • Products are used according to their label directions.
  • Application timing depends on weather and site conditions.
  • Spot spraying is performed in appropriate areas when needed.
  • Results can vary based on weed type, lawn condition, and other factors.

If you have questions about treatment for a particular area of your property, you can include those details in your quote request.
