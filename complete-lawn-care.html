Fertilization

Fertilization helps provide your lawn with nutrients that support healthy growth, color, and overall lawn condition. The timing and amount of fertilizer needed depend on the grass type, lawn condition, seasonal needs, and the product being used.

Bermuda grass changes its growth throughout the year. Fertilization should be adjusted to match its growing season rather than applied on the same schedule year-round.

Spring — After Green-Up

Once Bermuda grass has come out of dormancy and is actively growing, fertilization may help support its return to active growth. Timing depends on lawn conditions and the weather.

Summer — Active Growth

During active growth, Bermuda grass may benefit from additional nutrients when needed. Applications should account for the lawn's condition, watering, weather, and the product's directions.

Fall — Slowing Growth

As temperatures cool and growth slows, fertilization needs change. Late-season applications should be considered carefully instead of continuing a summer schedule automatically.

Winter — Dormant Grass

Dormant Bermuda grass generally does not need routine nitrogen fertilization. The next growing season's needs can be considered as the lawn approaches spring green-up.

Important: These are general seasonal guidelines, not a guaranteed application schedule. Fertilizer selection, timing, and amounts depend on lawn needs, weather, soil conditions, and product label directions.

Spot Spraying & Weed Control

Spot spraying focuses on localized areas where weeds are present. This can include appropriate areas in flower beds, sidewalk cracks, driveway cracks, and similar spots around the property.

Spot spraying is performed during each service visit when needed. We target appropriate areas where weeds are present rather than automatically spraying every area on every visit.

Please note: Spot spraying is not a whole-lawn weed control treatment. Treating weeds throughout an entire lawn is a different service and is not included in the spot-spraying portion of this plan.

Treatment Guidelines

Every lawn and property is different. Treatment decisions depend on the weeds present, grass and landscape conditions, weather, and the treatment product's intended use.

  • Products are used according to their label directions.
  • Application timing depends on weather and site conditions.
  • Not every area will require treatment during every visit.
  • Results can vary based on weed type, lawn condition, and other factors.

If you have questions about whether a particular lawn or landscape area needs treatment, you can include that information in your quote request.
