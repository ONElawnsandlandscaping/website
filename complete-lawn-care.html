Complete Lawn Care

Complete Lawn Care builds on our regular lawn maintenance services with fertilization and targeted spot spraying when needed. The goal is to help maintain a healthy-looking lawn while addressing weeds in specific areas.

Fertilization

Fertilization provides nutrients that help grass grow and maintain its color and density. The right timing and amount depend on the type of grass, its current condition, the weather, and the needs of the property.

For Bermuda grass in Oklahoma, fertilization is generally most useful while the grass is actively growing. Applications should be planned around the lawn's growth cycle rather than applied automatically at every time of year.

Seasonal Guidelines for Bermuda Grass

Spring

Begin considering fertilization after the lawn has greened up and is actively growing. Applying too early, before active growth, may be less effective.

Summer

Bermuda grass typically grows actively during warm weather. Fertilization may be appropriate when the lawn needs nutrients and conditions are suitable.

Fall

As growth slows and the lawn prepares for dormancy, reduce fertilization as appropriate. Avoid encouraging unnecessary late-season growth.

Winter

Bermuda grass is generally dormant during winter. Routine nitrogen fertilization is usually unnecessary while the grass is dormant.

Application Guidelines

Fertilizer type, application rate, and timing should be selected according to the lawn's needs and the product label. Weather, watering, soil conditions, and the condition of the grass can all affect the best time to apply fertilizer.

Spot Spraying & Weed Control

Spot spraying focuses on weeds in specific areas rather than treating the entire lawn. During a service visit, we can address visible weeds in appropriate locations when needed.

Where Spot Spraying May Be Used

  • Weeds growing in cracks along sidewalks and driveways.
  • Weeds in appropriate areas around flower beds.
  • Other suitable, localized areas where weeds are present.

Products are selected and used according to their labels and the location being treated. Care is taken around desirable plants, sensitive surfaces, and areas where a product should not be applied.

Please note: Spot spraying targets weeds in specific areas when needed during each service visit. We do not offer full-lawn weed control treatments.

Treatment Guidelines

Lawn treatments depend on the condition of the property, the type of grass, the weather, and the products being used. Not every lawn needs the same treatment or schedule.

  • Follow product labels. Application directions, rates, precautions, and restrictions must be followed.
  • Consider weather conditions. Rain, wind, heat, and watering can affect whether a treatment is appropriate.
  • Protect desirable plants. Spot treatments should be limited to appropriate target areas.
  • Assess the lawn first. Treatment needs can change as grass grows and seasonal conditions change.
