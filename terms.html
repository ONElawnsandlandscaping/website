Terms of Service

1. Scheduling and Weather Policy

Service dates may be adjusted due to weather, wet or unsafe lawn conditions, equipment issues, or other unforeseen circumstances. Services postponed due to weather or unsafe conditions will be rescheduled as soon as reasonably practical. ONE Lawns & Landscaping will notify customers of significant delays or schedule changes when possible.

2. Payment Policy

Payment is due according to the payment arrangement agreed upon when service is scheduled. A late fee of $10 per week will be charged on overdue balances, with the first $10 late fee applied seven days after the service is completed if payment has not been received. Additional late fees of $10 will be charged for each subsequent week the balance remains unpaid. ONE Lawns & Landscaping reserves the right to suspend or discontinue service for unpaid balances.

3. Service Cancellation and Skipped Visits

Customers requesting cancellation of recurring service should provide at least 24 hours' notice before their next scheduled visit whenever possible.

Customers may request up to two skipped mowing visits per growing season without affecting their regular service rate. This policy is intended to provide reasonable flexibility while discouraging customers from repeatedly skipping scheduled services to avoid paying while maintaining their recurring-service rate.

Additional skipped visits may result in a review of the regular service rate or additional charges if lawn conditions require extra work. Customers remain responsible for payment for completed services and any previously approved work or materials.

4. Property Access and Cleanup

Customers are responsible for providing safe and reasonable access to the property on scheduled service days. Gates should be unlocked, and vehicles, toys, hoses, pet waste, and other obstructions should be removed from work areas before service whenever possible. Locked gates, inaccessible areas, or other obstructions may result in delayed, skipped, or rescheduled service. Customers must secure pets before service begins.

If ONE Lawns & Landscaping must remove pet waste, pick up toys, move loose items, or perform other cleanup of customer-related obstructions before or during service, a $20 cleanup fee may be charged.

5. Lawn Conditions and Additional Charges

Standard pricing is based on normal lawn conditions and the scope of work agreed upon. Overgrown grass, excessive debris, excessive pet waste, unusually difficult terrain, or other conditions that increase labor or equipment use may result in additional charges. ONE Lawns & Landscaping will communicate additional charges and obtain customer approval before performing additional work whenever reasonably possible.

6. Damage and Property Conditions

Customers should notify ONE Lawns & Landscaping of underground utilities, irrigation components, hidden property features, loose items, and other hazards before service begins. Customers should mark concealed hazards when appropriate. ONE Lawns & Landscaping will take reasonable care while performing services. Any concerns about possible damage should be reported promptly so the circumstances can be reviewed.

7. Fertilization and Spot Spraying

Results from fertilization, spot spraying, and weed treatment may vary depending on weather, soil conditions, watering practices, lawn health, weed type, and application timing. ONE Lawns & Landscaping does not guarantee complete weed elimination or specific growth results. Customers should follow product recommendations provided for watering, lawn care, and other post-application instructions.

8. Landscaping Services and Estimates

Estimates are based on the requested scope of work, visible site conditions, and material prices available at the time of estimating. Additional work outside the approved estimate may result in additional charges, which will be discussed with the customer before proceeding.

Prices for mulch, decorative rock, sod, soil, plants, and other landscape materials may change before installation due to supplier pricing or availability. Plant, sod, and material warranties are limited to applicable manufacturer or supplier warranties. Customers are responsible for proper watering and ongoing maintenance after installation.

9. Recurring Lawn Maintenance and Mowing Guidelines

Recurring service may be offered on a weekly, biweekly, or another agreed schedule. Weekly service is generally recommended during periods of active growth.

To maintain lawn health, no more than one-third of the grass blade should be removed during a single mowing whenever possible. Excessively tall grass may require additional time, labor, or specialized work and may result in additional charges.

10. Property Photos and Privacy

ONE Lawns & Landscaping may take photographs of a customer's property and completed work for quality control, estimating, service documentation, service records, and marketing purposes. Personal information will never be shared in photographs. Property owners may opt out of having their property photographed by notifying ONE Lawns & Landscaping before service.

11. Right to Refuse or Discontinue Service

ONE Lawns & Landscaping may refuse, pause, or discontinue service when unsafe conditions, abusive behavior, nonpayment, repeated access problems, or other circumstances prevent safe and professional work. When practical, the customer will be notified of the reason. Any outstanding balance for completed work or approved materials remains due.
