Terms of Service

Terms of Service Scheduling Service dates may be adjusted due to weather, equipment issues, or other unforeseen circumstances. Customers will be notified of significant schedule changes when possible. Payment Payment is due upon completion of service unless other arrangements have been made. Late payments may be subject to additional fees. Service Cancellation Customers requesting cancellation of recurring service should provide at least 24 hours notice before their next scheduled visit. Property Access Customers are responsible for providing safe and reasonable access to the property on scheduled service days. Locked gates, pets, vehicles, or other obstructions may result in skipped or delayed service. Pet Safety Customers are responsible for securing pets during service visits. Lawn Conditions Pricing is based on normal lawn conditions. Overgrown grass, excessive debris, excessive pet waste, or unusually difficult conditions may result in additional charges. Customers will be notified of any additional charges before work is performed whenever possible. Damage & Liability ONE Lawns & Landscaping is not responsible for damage caused by hidden obstacles, pet toys, or other items not clearly visible before service. Customers are responsible for marking or notifying us of any concealed hazards. Weather Policy Services may be postponed due to unsafe weather conditions. Delayed services will be rescheduled as soon as practical. Customers will be notified of weather delays or reschedules. Fertilization & Weed Control Results may vary based on weather, soil conditions, watering practices, and existing lawn health. No guarantee is made regarding specific growth rates or weed elimination. Landscaping Services Plant, sod, mulch, and landscape material warranties are limited to manufacturer or supplier warranties, if applicable. Customers are responsible for proper watering and ongoing maintenance after installation. Estimates Additional work requested beyond the original estimate may result in additional charges. Material pricing for mulch, rock, sod, soil, and other landscape materials is subject to supplier price changes prior to installation. Recurring Lawn Maintenance Weekly service is recommended during the growing season. To maintain lawn health, no more than one-third of the grass blade should be removed during a single mowing whenever possible. Lawns requiring excessive height reduction may be subject to an overgrowth fee. Customers may request to skip up to two scheduled mowing visits per growing season without affecting their regular service rate. Once active growing season begins, additional skipped visits may result in a rate increase or overgrowth fee due to increased labor, mowing time, and equipment wear. Photo Permission ONE Lawns & Landscaping may photograph completed work for quality control, estimating, and marketing purposes. No personal information will be shared without customer consent. Right to Refuse Service ONE Lawns & Landscaping reserves the right to refuse or discontinue service due to unsafe conditions, abusive behavior, non-payment, or other circumstances that prevent safe and professional work.
